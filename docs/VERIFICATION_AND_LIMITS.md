# Verified Claims and Explicit Limits

## What the sealed release verifies

The final claim is scoped to the exact LayerCake product lineage, Qwen 2.5
0.5B comparator deployment, data, checkpoints, packages, runtime settings, and
laptop hardware named in the Phase 8 raw evidence.

- The full eight-phase campaign is sealed and independently reproducible from
  its raw evidence.
- LayerCake passed the locked 100-prompt functional suite on CPU and GPU and
  retained every promoted held-out domain result across CPU and GPU.
- LayerCake's output-byte throughput exceeded the locked optimized Qwen
  comparator by 9.91x on CPU and 8.25x on GPU. LayerCake CPU also exceeded the
  comparator's GPU throughput by 7.67x in the declared matrix.
- The final product installed, verified, removed, and reinstalled identical,
  signed, non-executable Python, SQL, and regular-expression capability archives
  on three fresh hosts without receiver training or calibration.
- Routing was archive-bound and fail-closed: a missing, stale, unauthenticated,
  mismatched, or permission-ineligible package cannot enter automatic execution.
- Only selected promoted capabilities execute. Core-only operation abstains
  safely when the request requires an unavailable capability.

## What the sealed release does not claim

The following remain intentionally outside the certificate:

- physical Android, iOS, ARM, NPU, battery, thermal, or mobile performance;
- calibrated energy or carbon dominance;
- GPU-training dominance;
- faster from-scratch English acquisition or a general 5x training claim;
- latent neural fusion of multiple direct-decoder cakes;
- universal language-model superiority beyond the frozen functional suites;
- arbitrary third-party host compatibility;
- external human, laboratory, or remote-repository independence;
- publication of the final Git tag to a remote.

The Phase 3 training-efficiency controls are preserved as historical science but
are not product gates. Its sealed retirement certificate has zero headline
claims and does not certify training efficiency, ABI bootstrapping, external
artifact acquisition, or LayerCake host certification. ABI extraction,
minimization, and source-model knowledge transfer are separate work and are not
implemented, imported, or certified by this LayerCake release.

## Correct interpretation of portability

Package transfer is mathematical at the archive and tensor boundary: the same
authenticated bytes are installed on compatible hosts. Semantic retention is
measured separately using frozen functional success sets. Neither property
authorizes a claim that arbitrary neural networks share representations or that
any package can run on any host.

## Correct interpretation of speed

The primary cross-model rate is UTF-8 output bytes per complete request wall
second. Transformer tokens are recorded through the deployment runtime but are
not equated with LayerCake actions. Quality, speed, memory, and latency use the
same accepted product lineage; values from an older checkpoint or another model
cannot be mixed into a release claim.

For command-level reproduction and all raw values, use the certificates and
raw files under `results/moonshot/phase8/`, not this summary.
