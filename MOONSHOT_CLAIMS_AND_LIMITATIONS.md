# Moonshot Claims and Limitations

The Moonshot campaign is sealed at `layercake-moonshot-final`. The canonical
claim map is [CLAIMS.md](CLAIMS.md); the detailed boundaries are
[docs/VERIFICATION_AND_LIMITS.md](docs/VERIFICATION_AND_LIMITS.md); and the
phase-by-phase evidence is [docs/PHASE_STATUS.md](docs/PHASE_STATUS.md).

expand the final release. Those experiments remain available as historical
positive and negative evidence only.
